Expense Wave — Delete Your Account

App: Expense Wave  ·  Developer: Amuse Apps Lab

This page explains how to request deletion of your Expense Wave account and the data associated with it. You can delete your account directly in the app, or request deletion by email.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

1. Open Expense Wave and go to Settings.
2. Tap Delete account.
3. Confirm. Your account and personal data are removed right away, and you are signed out.

Option 2 — Request by email

Send an email to amuseappslab@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my Expense Wave account". We verify the request and delete your data within 7 days.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your profile — name, email address and avatar received from Google Sign-In
  • All personal entries — expenses, income, categories, notes and salary-cycle settings
  • Your Circle memberships and your personal balances
  • Your device push-notification token

What may be kept

Expenses you added inside a shared Circle may be retained for the other members of that Circle so their shared balances remain accurate. Where retained, these entries are disassociated from your profile (your name and avatar are removed). If you want a specific shared entry removed, delete it in the app before deleting your account, or ask the Circle owner.

Retention period

Personal data is deleted immediately (in-app) or within 7 days (email request). Residual copies in encrypted backups are purged within 30 days. Expense Wave does not process payments, so we keep no financial-transaction records for legal retention.
